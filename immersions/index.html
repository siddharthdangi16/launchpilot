Questions

Before you express interest.

Who is this actually for?▾
Founders, operators, and business owners who are actively building — not observers. Each immersion is curated around the kind of person the local founders and investors actually want in the room, so we do review every application.
Can I do more than one immersion?▾
Yes — most alumni do. Many founders use immersions to explore multiple markets before deciding where to actually expand, and the alumni network spans every cohort across all nine countries.
What's actually included?▾
Curated 1:1 and small-group intros to founders, mentors, angel investors, and VCs in that market, plus group sessions, site visits, and the cohort itself. Flights and accommodation are quoted separately based on your city of departure and preferences.
How big is each cohort?▾
Deliberately small — 12 to 16 founders per immersion. Small enough that every intro is real, not shared across a crowd.
When does the next cohort start?▾
Cohorts run on a rolling basis throughout the year for each city. Express interest and we'll tell you exactly when the next available seat is for the immersion you want.

The Journey

Six cities.
Two months each.

You don't visit these cities — you live and build in them. Each one has a curriculum theme designed around what that city does best.

Month 01–02
Singapore
Southeast Asia's startup capital
Foundation & AI
Where Asia's future is being built. You set up your company, deploy your first AI systems, and immerse yourself in the world's most efficient business ecosystem.
AI strategy, product development, and go-to-market fundamentals
Company incorporation, equity, and legal structure
First paying users — before you leave Singapore
Deliverable → Live AI-powered product with first paying users
Month 03–04
Tokyo
The land of craft and obsessive execution
Product Excellence
You study kaizen, iterate your product with precision, and learn what world-class execution really looks like. Tokyo is where your product becomes something people love.
Kaizen methodology — obsessive product iteration
Design thinking and world-class user experience
First international market entry and localisation
Deliverable → V2 product with Japanese market traction
Month 05–06
London
Europe's financial and startup capital
Fundraising & Capital
You raise your first round, navigate regulatory landscapes, and pitch to the most sophisticated investors in Europe. London is where your company gets funded.
VC, angel, and alternative fundraising — live pitches
Financial modelling, unit economics, and investor narrative
European regulatory environment and expansion strategy
Deliverable → Completed funding round or signed term sheet
Month 07–08
New York
The world's most competitive market
Scale & Distribution
The biggest market, the most demanding customers, and the best growth minds on the planet. New York is where your company learns to scale without breaking.
Performance marketing, growth loops, and viral mechanics
Brand architecture, PR, and US media positioning
Enterprise sales operations and high-ticket deal-making
Deliverable → US market entry with active customer pipeline
Month 09–10
Dubai
Wealth, networks, and cross-border expansion
Wealth & Networks
The Gulf is where fortunes are made at a speed Europe and the US can't match. You build relationships with HNIs, family offices, and sovereign investors who don't respond to cold emails.
HNI and family office relationships — warm intros only
MENA market entry and UAE free zone strategy
Wealth management, exits, and secondary transactions
Deliverable → MENA strategy + HNI investor relationships
Month 11–12
Mumbai
The world's fastest-growing economy
Legacy & Launch
You cap 12 months of building with India — the most exciting market on the planet. Global Demo Day brings investors from all six cities into one room.
Executive leadership, culture design, and org systems
India market strategy — consumer and B2B
Global Demo Day + MBA thesis and degree completion
Deliverable → Global company with 6 markets, a team, and live revenue

  • Show trajectory — if it was 1.8x six months ago, that's the story
  • Break by channel — if one channel hits 5x, lead with that
  • Lead with payback — if it's under 18 months, that's stronger

The Problem

Sport asks people
to choose.

Play or study. Compete or build a career. For most people who fall in love with a sport, that's the trade they're handed — often before they're old enough to understand what they're giving up.

The ones who choose sport give it everything. Then the sport ends — through injury, age, or simply not making the cut — and they're left with no qualification built for what comes next, no professional network outside the locker room, no roadmap. That isn't a personal failure. It's a gap nobody built the bridge for. Athlo is that bridge.

"The discipline, focus and resilience of an athlete is exactly what every employer wants. The problem was never the athlete — it's the missing bridge."

$500B+

Size of the global sports industry today — and still only a fraction of people working in it are athletes.

27–33

The age range most professional athletes retire at, decades before a typical career even peaks.

1000s

Of commercial, analytics, media and operations roles behind every league — with no structured path in for athletes or fans.

Sources: The Business Research Company, sports industry retirement-age reporting

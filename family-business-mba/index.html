The Only Two Outcomes That Matter

Everything in this program
serves one of two goals.

No general management theory, no ten-item wishlist. Every module, every mentor session, every week abroad exists to move you toward one of these two outcomes — and you don't graduate until both are real.

Outcome I
Take the business into one new international market.
Leave with a signed LOI, distributor agreement, or partnership conversation actively underway in a market you chose — not a 40-page strategy deck that sits in a drawer. The offline week each month happens in the market itself.
Market selection & entry-mode decision (partner, JV, subsidiary, distributor)
Regulatory, trade, and tax landscape for your chosen market
Introductions to real distributors, partners, or buyers on the ground
A 12-month go-to-market plan your team can execute after graduation
Outcome II
Cut real cost from the business using AI.
Leave with two or three processes actually automated — not a slide about "AI transformation." A quantified, implemented reduction in operating cost, built for your specific business, not a generic tool demo.
A function-by-function audit of where AI removes real cost or time
2–3 processes automated and live before graduation (ops, finance, or customer-facing)
A quantified savings figure your board or family can see
Your existing team trained to run and extend the systems after you leave

How The Six Months Work

Three weeks at your desk.

Every month follows the exact same rhythm — three weeks of live cohort work from home, then one week physically in that month's target market. It repeats six times, in a different city each time.

Curriculum

Built backward from
the two outcomes.

Six modules, each ladders directly into either the market-entry outcome or the AI cost-reduction outcome. Nothing here is generic MBA filler.

Module 01
Business & Market Diagnosis
▾
A structured audit of your business as it stands today — where margin leaks, where the org chart hides risk, and which international market actually fits your product, not just the market that sounds impressive.
Module 02
Market Entry Strategy
▾
Entry-mode decisions (distributor, JV, subsidiary), regulatory and trade landscape, and how to structure a first deal so it survives contact with a new legal system and culture.
Module 03
AI Cost & Process Audit
▾
A function-by-function walkthrough of your operations to find where AI genuinely removes cost or headcount need — not a tour of tools you'll never touch again.
Module 04
Implementation & Build
▾
Hands-on build week — your first automated processes go live with a mentor beside you, and your existing team is trained to maintain and extend them.
Module 05
Deal & Partnership Close
▾
Negotiation, term sheets, and closing mechanics for the specific partner or distributor relationship you're forming in your target market.
Module 06
Board Presentation & Handover
▾
You present both outcomes — market entry status and quantified AI savings — to your own family or board, the way you'd present any real initiative.

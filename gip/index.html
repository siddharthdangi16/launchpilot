FAQ

Questions we
always get asked.

Do I need to quit my job to join? +
No. The Global Innovators Program is designed to run alongside your job. It requires 10–15 hours a week — Sunday Labs, your AI Copilot sessions, and sprint work. Most of our working professional cohort keep their jobs until they have real traction, then decide.
Do I need an idea before I apply? +
Not necessarily. Sprint 01 is specifically designed to help you validate, sharpen, or find a better idea. Many of our best companies came from founders who scrapped their original idea in week 3 and found something real. What matters is that you have a space you're curious about and the drive to build.
Do I need to be based in Singapore? +
The Sunday Labs are in Singapore and we strongly recommend being here — the city's startup ecosystem is a core part of the program. However, the curriculum and AI Copilot are fully online. We've had founders join from India, Indonesia, Dubai, and the UK who flew in for key sessions and Demo Day.
What is the time commitment? +
Roughly 10–15 hours per week: Sunday Labs (3–4 hours live), AI Copilot sessions (2–3 hours), and sprint work (5–8 hours). The program is intense by design — building a company while working isn't easy, and we don't pretend it is. But 6 months is a very short time to go from idea to Demo Day.
What does the AI Copilot actually do? +
It's a personalised AI trained on everything LaunchPilot knows about building startups — and it learns your specific context during onboarding. Your ICP, your business model, your stage, your blockers. So when you ask "how should I price this?", the answer is about your product, not a generic framework. It's available 24/7 and gets smarter the more you use it.
Can I find a co-founder through the program? +
Yes — and it happens all the time. Your cohort of 30 includes technical founders, operators, marketers, and domain experts. We actively facilitate introductions between complementary founders during Sprint 01. Some of the strongest companies to come out of LaunchPilot were co-founded by people who met in the program.
What happens after Demo Day? +
You join the LaunchPilot × NUS alumni network of 700+ founders globally. If you're raising, we facilitate continued investor introductions. If you're hiring, the network is your talent pool. Many graduates come back as mentors for future cohorts. The program ends — the community doesn't.
Is $1M in startup benefits real? +
Yes. AWS credits ($5,000), OpenAI API credits ($2,500), Stripe fee-free processing ($20,000), HubSpot (90% off), Vercel, Supabase, Notion, Linear, Apollo, Webflow — and 490+ more tools and discounts. These are real credits, not trial accounts. The same stack that funded startups run on, from day one.

Questions

Before you reserve a seat.

Do I need to be a founder to take these?▾
No. These courses are built for anyone who wants to go deep on a specific, advanced skill — operators, investors-in-training, product managers, corporate finance professionals. The material assumes competence, not a specific job title.
Are these actually advanced, or beginner content in disguise?▾
Advanced. Every course assumes you already understand the fundamentals of the subject and starts from there — no "introduction to" material. If a course isn't a fit for your level, we'll tell you before you enroll.
What happens if I miss a live session?▾
Sessions are recorded for enrolled participants to catch up, but the format is built around live participation — most of the value is in the real-time discussion, not the recording.
How are cohorts kept so small?▾
Every course is capped at 15-20 seats and applications are reviewed — we're not optimizing for enrollment volume, we're optimizing for a room the mentor can actually engage with.
When does the next cohort start?▾
Cohorts run on a rolling basis throughout the year. Reserve a seat for the course you want and we'll confirm the exact start date for the next available cohort.
